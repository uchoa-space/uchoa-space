I claimed an agent runtime with supervisor-owned gates. A claim like that is cheap; what makes it checkable is a repository where the gates are files, the authors are dated, and the supervisor's decision is a line you can `git blame`. This post is that repository, read once. One wave on one project: it proves the mechanism ran, and nothing about whether it pays.

**TL;DR**

- The bus is a directory, [`bus/v1/`](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/tree/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1), committed with the code. Six authors, sixty-six entries on the three threads that matter, each with a timestamp and a sequence number.
- The supervisor, signed `admiral`, wrote twenty-one entries. All are comments. Three boundaries: the intent, eighteen record picks, one verdict.
- The wave merged at [`61c7e94`](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/commit/61c7e94bf2f1e45840131ade7be38314be21d930) after a review by a second author who re-ran the gate script and posted the output.
- The gate is a brief and one command. The supervisor never opened the code.
- N=1, no conclusion. Two escapes and one refusal are on the record, and the brief got the refusal count wrong.

## What was asked

Thread 1 opens with an intent posted by `kirk` and answered by `admiral` in one entry: outcome, user, why now, success, constraints. The success line is the thesis, from [`threads/001/admiral.md`](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/001/admiral.md), seq 4:

> wave 1 closes with the Admiral touching only three boundaries (intent, ADR selection, wave review); every extra entry is counted and becomes an ADR; the three counters (hit rate, escapes, override rate) come out instrumented; nothing is concluded from N=1.

The wave is small on purpose: derive one use-case header, `Chat::Open`, as a Ruby 3.3 stdlib kernel, prove it with a suite and a `bin/ci` script, and render the header back out with `bin/rita explain`.

## What the bus shows

**Boundary one.** `admiral` answered the intent (seq 4) and confirmed the analyst's seven guesses with one word, `yes` (seq 5). Nothing else from that author on the thread until the verdict.

**Boundary two.** On [thread 3](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/003/admiral.md), `spock` shortlisted the decision records and named eight gaps; `admiral` answered with eighteen one-line entries, sixteen tagged `adr.hit` and two `adr.miss`. Those tags are the hit rate's numerator and denominator; nobody typed the fraction.

**The build.** On thread 4, `scotty` derived the header, then wrote the two things that were not derived as [escapes](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/004/scotty.md), seq 5 and 6, each with a `because`: a hand-written status predicate, and a hand-kept port method list. The evidence entry names the sha, `ba79776`, and the run: 19 runs, 81 assertions, 0 failures.

**The review.** `bones` re-ran `bin/ci` at [`ba79776`](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/commit/ba79776a18fbf25ed1c43b8f26110edd116ec202) in a detached worktree, read the whole diff, and [approved](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/004/bones.md) with four non-blocking findings, carried to the next wave rather than fixed. `kirk` merged. The wave diff is [`86c071c...61c7e94`](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/compare/86c071cd938192798388476d513bda78360f1b63...61c7e94bf2f1e45840131ade7be38314be21d930): 21 files, 656 lines, of which 175 are kernel code.

**Boundary three.** `uhura` wrote the [gate brief](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/001/uhura.md) at seq 19: mission, wave goal, what was proven, what was argued not measured, escapes and refusals, a recommendation, and the exact command. The supervisor's entire third boundary is seq 21, tag `verdict`:

```
accept wave 1 at 61c7e94bf2f1e45840131ade7be38314be21d930
```

<mark>The reviewer had opened the code; the supervisor had not.</mark> That is the design; the bus is what lets you check it held.

## The header, rendered

`bin/rita explain` at the merge sha prints the header the wave was about. This is copied from the [wave record](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/docs/decisions/003-wave-1-chat-open.md), not retyped:

```
Chat::Open  command
  intent       Open a thread for the reader to talk in
  leaves       thread: open
  invalidates  thread
  effects      thread -> open; stale: thread

stages: call -> leaves -> invalidates
words: intent 1, accepts 0, requires 0, leaves 1, returns 0, invalidates 1, once 0, renders 0
```

One command, no inputs, one postcondition the kernel verifies after the call rather than performs: the smallest header that exercises the rule.

## The counters

From the same record, `bus metrics` at merge, verbatim, under the line the record carries:

N=1, no conclusion.

```
hit_rate: 16/18 = 0.89
escapes: 2 (2/1 = 2.00 over derived)
override_rate: 0/0 = —
events: 125
```

<Callout>
The escapes ratio has a denominator of one, because the wave had one derived entry. A ratio of 2.00 is the definition not meaning anything yet, not a finding. The override rate has no denominator at all: the supervisor was never refused, so there was nothing to override.
</Callout>

## What went wrong, on the record

The gate brief says "Refusals: none on threads 1 or 4." The event log disagrees: one guard refusal, when a review dispatch was killed from outside before the reviewer wrote anything ([thread 4](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/bus/v1/threads/004/kirk.md), seq 11, exit 1), plus three refused runs from mistyped arguments. The [acceptance record](https://github.com/uchoa-space/rita/blob/dba5c749defe4bc13fd0f4e65c45bc272b098b7f/docs/decisions/004-wave-1-accepted-by-the-admiral.md) names the error and its cause: the brief reported a counter the metrics query does not carry, read from the threads by eye. The same record corrects `kirk`, who cited the verdict at seq 20; seq 20 is a dispatch. The bus wins over the comment.

That is the whole exhibit: a public directory, six authors, three boundaries, one line. Whether the mechanism is worth its cost is a question for N≥2; the counters exist so it can be asked.
